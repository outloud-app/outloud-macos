# Platform marks

Reddit, Instagram, X and TikTok glyphs are sourced from Simple Icons:

https://github.com/simple-icons/simple-icons/tree/develop/icons

The original SVGs are retained here. App display assets use the same vector paths with recognizable brand-color treatments and are bundled in the asset catalog. These identify the source platform; no partnership or platform integration is implied.

Simple Icons distributes its icons under CC0. Brand names and marks remain the property of their respective owners.
